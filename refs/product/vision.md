# Product Vision

Provide a guided ComfyUI workflow that turns a song and landscape reference
artwork into an exact-duration 1080p motion poster suitable for casual YouTube
listening. The workflow prioritizes fidelity to the supplied composition over
cinematic novelty: subtle environmental motion, stable identity, no accidental
lip sync, deterministic assembly, and post-generation branding.

The ordinary operator should select media, adjust plain-language guidance,
optionally enable a logo and wordmark, and queue once. Manifests and command-line
rendering remain reproducibility and diagnostic tools rather than required user
steps.

The accepted baseline deliberately uses one short native-landscape Wan asset,
neutral camera motion, bounded-memory FFmpeg looping, exact audio duration, and
Lanczos 1080p delivery. Small or off-center faces, multi-variant transitions,
beat effects, timed lyrics, multi-reference conditioning, and AI upscaling are
future opportunities rather than claims of the current product.
